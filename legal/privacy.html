1. What we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, email, phone, company, and whether you are a dealer, auction, fleet, manufacturer or individual.
  • Transport details: pickup and delivery addresses, dates, vehicle year, make, model, VIN, condition, damage notes and any photos you upload.
  • Payment details: handled entirely by Stripe. We receive only a payment reference, the card brand and the last four digits — never your full card number.
  • Agreement records: when you sign the pickup confirmation we store the agreement text shown, your typed name, the date and time, your IP address and browser type, as proof of what was agreed.
  • Account details for Exchange users: login email, password (stored only as a one-way hash), company, and the loads you post or accept.
  • Technical data: IP address and basic request logs, used for security and to keep the Site running.
  • Email address only if you sign up for updates.

2. How we use it

  • To quote, book, dispatch, deliver and bill your transport, and to contact you about it.
  • To place and manage card holds and charges you have authorized, and to keep proof of agreements.
  • To run the Exchange load board for carriers and shippers.
  • To protect the Site against fraud and abuse.
  • To send occasional updates if you asked for them. You can stop these at any time by replying or emailing us.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

3. Who we share it with

Only the companies we need to run the service, and only what they need:

  • Stripe — payment processing and secure card storage.
  • Carriers assigned to your transport — your name, phone, addresses and vehicle details so they can pick up and deliver.
  • Google Maps — address suggestions and mileage on our forms.
  • Resend — sends our emails.
  • Amazon Web Services — stores uploaded photos and documents.
  • Railway and Cloudflare — host and protect the Site and route our email.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our customers or our carriers.

4. How long we keep it

  • Card details: kept by Stripe for up to 7 days for a pickup confirmation, then removed automatically, or immediately after the transport or fee is charged.
  • Orders, agreements and payment records: kept for as long as needed for accounting, tax and dispute purposes, generally 7 years.
  • Exchange accounts: until you ask us to close the account.
  • Update-list emails: until you unsubscribe.

5. Cookies

We use one cookie to keep you signed in to the admin panel or your Exchange account. It contains no personal data beyond a signed login token. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Google Maps may set its own cookies when it loads on our forms; see Google's privacy policy for those.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, unless we must keep it for legal or accounting reasons.
  • You can ask us to release a card hold at any time before dispatch.
  • You can close an Exchange account by emailing us.

Email sales@mcships.com for any of these. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

All traffic to the Site is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored hashed. Card numbers never touch our servers. Access to customer records is limited to MC staff who need it. No system is perfectly secure, so if you believe your information has been exposed, please tell us right away.

8. Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version. Significant changes will be noted on the Site.
