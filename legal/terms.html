3. Payment

  • Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Website bookings are charged at checkout.
  • Phone-in bookings use a card authorization: when you confirm the pickup we place a temporary hold for the quoted amount. Nothing is charged until the vehicle is picked up. Your bank may show the hold as "pending"; that is normal.
  • Your card details are kept securely by Stripe for up to 7 days for the pickup, then removed automatically. Once the transport (or a fee under section 5) is charged, the card is removed.
  • If a hold expires before pickup, we will ask you to confirm again before we dispatch.
  • Charges appear on your statement as MCSHIPS.

4. Pickup confirmation and agreement

For phone-in bookings we email you a confirmation link. On that page you review the order, read the pickup agreement, type your name to sign it, and authorize your card. That signed agreement is part of these Terms, and we keep a record of exactly what you agreed to (the text shown, your name, the date and time, and the network address used) so both sides have proof.

5. Cancellations, no-shows and dry-run fees

  • You may cancel a phone-in booking at no cost any time before we dispatch a carrier and with at least 24 hours' notice before the scheduled pickup. Ask us to release the hold; nothing is charged.
  • If our carrier arrives and the vehicle is not available — for example it was given to another transport company, sold, moved, is inaccessible, or nobody can release it — or if you cancel with less than 24 hours' notice after dispatch, a no-show / dry-run fee applies. The amount is stated on your quote and confirmation page (typically $150) and may be charged to the card on file.
  • Website bookings cancelled before dispatch are refunded in full. Cancellations after dispatch may be subject to the same dry-run fee.
  • Refunds go back to the original card and can take 5–10 business days to appear, depending on your bank.

6. Your responsibilities

  • Provide accurate vehicle, address and contact details, and tell us about damage, modifications (lifts, oversized tires, racks), and whether the vehicle runs, steers, brakes and rolls.
  • Make sure the vehicle is ready: keys available, no more than about a quarter tank of fuel, alarms disabled, loose parts secured, and accessible for a large truck.
  • Remove personal belongings. Carriers do not insure personal items left in vehicles and may refuse loads with excessive contents.
  • Be present, or have an authorized adult present, at pickup and delivery to inspect the vehicle and sign the Bill of Lading.
  • Confirm you own the vehicle or are authorized by the owner to ship it.

7. Inspection, damage and claims

  • The vehicle's condition is recorded on the Bill of Lading (BOL) at pickup and again at delivery. Inspect the vehicle carefully at delivery and note any new damage on the BOL before signing. Damage not noted on the delivery BOL is very difficult to claim.
  • Transport damage is covered by the carrier's cargo insurance. To make a claim, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with the signed BOL and photos. We will help you file with the carrier's insurer.
  • Normal road wear (dust, minor debris marks), pre-existing damage, mechanical failures unrelated to transport, and damage to personal items are not covered.
